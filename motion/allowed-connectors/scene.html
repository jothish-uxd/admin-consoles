Allow any server?

People could connect the AI to servers nobody at Echo Base has checked. A rogue server can:

  • read everything the AI sends it
  • hide instructions in its tool descriptions
  • change its tools after it’s added

People must accept these risks before adding a server, and every server added is logged.
